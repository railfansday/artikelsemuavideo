According to recent reports, tiny, a 12-year-old schoolgirl, is an ecojustice influencer, whose funny and disconcerting takes on the climate crisis go viral. She is also an alien, sent to warn humankind of its impending doom – but her mission is far more difficult than she anticipated.

This is the plot of Tiny Alarms, the first winner of Shakespeare’s Globe’s upcoming climate playwriting prize. It was inspired by the real beliefs of schoolchildren for whom its author, Timothy X Atack, ran creative writing workshops exploring ideas around the far future.
