Police are working with agencies across Europe after similar incidents occurred at two other Don Toliver concerts.

A group of organised criminals are believed to have carried out robberies during Don Toliver’s Manchester concert, where an “unknown substance” was released, police have said, with similar incidents having occurred at another two of the US rapper’s gigs in Europe.

Greater Manchester police remarked they were working to identify the suspects and the substance involved during the incident at the Co-op Live Arena on Sunday evening.
