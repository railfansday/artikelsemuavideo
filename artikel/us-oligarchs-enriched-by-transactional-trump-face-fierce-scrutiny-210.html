Democrats pledge aggressive investigations into Trump-allied billionaires and how president’s policies have boosted their bottom lines.

But with Democrats winning control of Congress in the midterms, House and Senate investigations are being planned for 2027 into several Maga oligarchs in hi-tech and crypto industries, examining how their firms and the president benefited from his “transactional” governing style, according to Democratic lawmakers and scholars.
