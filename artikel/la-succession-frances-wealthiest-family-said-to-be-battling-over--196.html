Bernard Arnault’s children – described as ‘modern-day French royals’ – are subject of rivalry rumours over who will succeed him.

They are France’s richest family, leading the world’s biggest luxury goods empire, dressed in matching Dior suits – a clan so powerful they have been described as modern-day French royals.

But in recent months, the family dynasty of Bernard Arnault, 77, the billionaire head of the LVMH group whose brands range from Louis Vuitton handbags to Tiffany jewellery, has become the subject of speculation about internal rivalry over which of his children could take over the company.
